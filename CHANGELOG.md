# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- A divider in the menu bar: the icons left of it are tucked away, the rest stay in view.
- Shown, In Panel or Hidden for every icon, and Apply, which ⌘-drags the icons to their side of the divider.
- A panel with the tucked-away icons when you point at Paravent’s icon. Clicking one opens its menu in the menu bar and tucks it away again when the menu closes.
- Update check: once a day Paravent asks GitHub for the latest version number, and nothing about you or your Mac is sent. Download and Install verifies the disk image, opens it and quits Paravent so you can drag the new version to Applications.
- English and Turkish.
